Our story

A family
tradition alive

Lutong Cebu was born in 2009 from a grandmother's kitchen in the heart of Cebu City. What started as a small carinderya serving the neighborhood has grown into a beloved dining destination — but the heart has never changed.

We source every ingredient fresh from local Cebu markets each morning. No shortcuts. No compromises. Just the same love and craft that has fed families in this city for over fifteen years.
